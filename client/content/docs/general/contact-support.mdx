# Contact & Support

We're here to help! Whether you have a question, need technical support, or want to provide feedback, there are several ways to reach us.

## Getting Help

Before reaching out, check these resources:

1. **This Documentation** — Open **Docs** in your business dashboard for full guides.
2. **[FAQ](./faq)** — Answers to the most frequently asked questions.
3. **In-app Help** — Look for tooltips and help text throughout the platform (including the docs assistant where available).

## Pilot support

The supervised pilot uses the support channel agreed directly with the venue.
Crowbar does not currently expose a public contact form or promise a response
time inside the product.

## Reporting Issues

If you encounter a bug or technical issue:

1. Note what you were trying to do when the issue occurred.
2. Take a screenshot if possible.
3. Send the pilot contact:
   - A description of the problem
   - Steps to reproduce the issue
   - Your browser and device information
   - Any error messages you saw

## Feedback & Suggestions

We love hearing from our users! If you have ideas for improving Crowbar:

- Send the agreed pilot contact what is working well and what could be better.
