# Contact & Support

We're here to help! Whether you have a question, need technical support, or want to provide feedback, there are several ways to reach us.

## Getting Help

Before reaching out, check these resources:

1. **This Documentation** — Use the search bar at the top to find answers to common questions.
2. **[FAQ](/docs/general/faq)** — Answers to the most frequently asked questions.
3. **In-app Help** — Look for tooltips and help text throughout the platform.

## Contact Us

If you can't find what you're looking for, get in touch:

### Contact Form

Use the **Contact Us** button found on the homepage or in the footer. Fill in:
- Your name
- Email address
- Subject
- Your message

We aim to respond to all inquiries within 24 hours.

### Email Support

Reach us directly at our support email for:
- Technical issues
- Account problems
- Billing questions
- Feature requests

## Reporting Issues

If you encounter a bug or technical issue:

1. Note what you were trying to do when the issue occurred.
2. Take a screenshot if possible.
3. Contact us with:
   - A description of the problem
   - Steps to reproduce the issue
   - Your browser and device information
   - Any error messages you saw

## Feedback & Suggestions

We love hearing from our users! If you have ideas for improving RK Reservations:

- Use the contact form to submit feature requests
- Let us know what's working well and what could be better
- Share your experience to help us improve the platform for everyone
