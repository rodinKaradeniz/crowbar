# Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to the most common questions about RK Reservations.

## General

### What is RK Reservations?

RK Reservations is an all-in-one platform that connects businesses and customers through a simple, intuitive reservation system. Businesses can manage their bookings, schedules, and customers, while customers can easily find and book appointments.

### Is RK Reservations free to use?

- **For customers**: Yes! Creating an account and making reservations is completely free.
- **For businesses**: We offer different pricing plans to suit your needs. Visit our [For Businesses](/for-businesses) page for details.

### What types of businesses can use RK Reservations?

RK Reservations is designed for any business that takes appointments or reservations, including:
- Restaurants and cafes
- Hair salons and barbershops
- Spas and wellness centers
- Medical and dental offices
- Consulting firms
- Fitness studios
- And many more!

## Customer Questions

### How do I make a reservation?

Visit a business's reservation page, select a service type, choose your preferred date and time, and confirm your booking. See our [Making a Reservation](/docs/customers/making-a-reservation) guide for detailed steps.

### Can I cancel or modify my reservation?

Yes, you can view, modify, or cancel your reservations at any time from your dashboard. Go to **Reservations** and click on the booking you want to change. See [Managing Reservations](/docs/customers/managing-reservations) for more details.

### How do I know if my reservation is confirmed?

After submitting a reservation, you can check its status in your **Reservations** or **Requests** section. Statuses include Pending, Confirmed, Rejected, Cancelled, and Completed.

### I forgot my password. What do I do?

Go to the [login page](/auth/login), click **Forgot Password**, and enter your email address. You'll receive a link to reset your password.

## Business Questions

### How do I set up my business profile?

After registering as a business, go to **Profile** in the sidebar to set up your business information, hours, service types, and booking settings. See [Getting Started as a Business](/docs/businesses/getting-started) for a complete guide.

### Can I have multiple staff members manage my business?

Yes! You can add staff members with different roles (Owner, Manager, Staff) to help manage your business. See [Staff Management](/docs/businesses/staff-management) for details.

### How do I manage incoming reservation requests?

Go to **Requests** in the sidebar to see pending reservations. You can accept or reject each request. Alternatively, enable auto-accept in your booking settings to automatically confirm all reservations. See [Handling Reservations](/docs/businesses/handling-reservations).

### Can customers pay online?

Yes, RK Reservations supports online payments through Stripe. You can configure payment settings for your service types to require deposits or full payment at booking time.

## Technical Questions

### What browsers are supported?

RK Reservations works on all modern browsers, including:
- Google Chrome
- Mozilla Firefox
- Safari
- Microsoft Edge

### Is my data secure?

Yes. We use industry-standard security practices, including:
- Encrypted passwords (bcrypt hashing)
- Secure authentication (JWT tokens)
- HTTPS encryption for all data in transit
- Secure payment processing through Stripe

### Can I access RK Reservations on my phone?

Yes! The platform is fully responsive and works great on mobile devices, tablets, and desktops.
