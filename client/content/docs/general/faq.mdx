# Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to the most common questions about Crowbar.

## General

### What is Crowbar?

Crowbar is a multi-module platform for service businesses. Depending on which modules your business has enabled, you can manage reservations, run a walk-in queue, operate a QR-based ordering system with a live kitchen/bar ticket board, track inventory, and access ML-powered analytics — all from one dashboard.

### Is Crowbar free to use?

- **For customers**: Yes! Creating an account and making reservations is completely free.
- **For businesses**: We offer different pricing plans to suit your needs. Visit our [For Businesses](/for-businesses) page for details.

### What types of businesses can use Crowbar?

Crowbar is designed for any business that takes appointments or reservations, including:
- Restaurants and cafes
- Hair salons and barbershops
- Spas and wellness centers
- Medical and dental offices
- Consulting firms
- Fitness studios
- And many more!

## Customer Questions

### How do I make a reservation?

Visit a business's reservation page, select a service type, choose your preferred date and time, and confirm your booking. See our [Making a Reservation](../customers/making-a-reservation) guide for detailed steps.

### Can I cancel or modify my reservation?

Yes, you can view, modify, or cancel your reservations at any time from your dashboard. Go to **Reservations** and click on the booking you want to change. See [Managing Reservations](../customers/managing-reservations) for more details.

### How do I know if my reservation is confirmed?

After submitting a reservation, you can check its status in your **Reservations** or **Requests** section. Statuses include Pending, Confirmed, Rejected, Cancelled, and Completed. You may also receive a **confirmation email** with a calendar invite when email is enabled for the business.

### I forgot my password. What do I do?

Go to the [login page](/auth/login), click **Forgot Password**, and enter your email address. You'll receive a link to reset your password.

## Business Questions

### How do I set up my business profile?

After registering as a business, go to **Profile** in the sidebar to set up your business information, hours, service types, and booking settings. See [Getting Started as a Business](../businesses/getting-started) for a complete guide.

### Can I have multiple staff members manage my business?

Yes! You can add staff members with different roles (Owner, Manager, Staff) to help manage your business. See [Staff Management](../businesses/staff-management) for details.

### How do I manage incoming reservation requests?

Go to **Requests** in the sidebar to see pending reservations. You can accept or reject each request. You can also mark individual **service types** to require approval (pending) or confirm immediately—see [Handling Reservations](../businesses/handling-reservations).

### Do customers get email when they book?

When outbound email is configured for your deployment, customers receive confirmation messages that can include reservation details and an **ICS** calendar file. See [Handling Reservations](../businesses/handling-reservations).

### How do I enable or disable modules?

Go to **Settings → Modules** in your dashboard. Each module (Queue, Ordering, Inventory, Insights) has a toggle. Changes take effect immediately — enabled modules appear in the sidebar, disabled ones are hidden. The Reservations module is always enabled.

### What are the available modules?

| Module | What it does |
|--------|-------------|
| **Reservations** | Online booking, schedule management, reservation analytics |
| **Queue** | Digital walk-in queue — customers join via QR, staff manage a live board |
| **Ordering** | QR menu, customer self-order, kitchen/bar live ticket board |
| **Inventory** | Stock tracking, par levels, movement history, low-stock alerts |
| **Insights** | ML demand forecasts, customer segmentation, cancellation risk, operational KPIs |

### What are Insights?

**Insights** runs **machine learning** models on your data for customer segmentation, cancellation risk scoring, and short-term demand forecasting. It also surfaces operational KPIs (reservations, ordering, inventory) computed from the last 30 days of live data. See the [Insights](../businesses/insights) guide.

## Technical Questions

### What browsers are supported?

Crowbar works on all modern browsers, including:
- Google Chrome
- Mozilla Firefox
- Safari
- Microsoft Edge

### Is my data secure?

Yes. We use industry-standard security practices, including:
- Encrypted passwords (bcrypt hashing)
- Secure authentication (JWT tokens)
- HTTPS encryption for all data in transit

### Can I access Crowbar on my phone?

Yes! The platform is fully responsive and works great on mobile devices, tablets, and desktops.
