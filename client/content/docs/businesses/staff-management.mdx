# Staff Management

If you have a team, you can add staff members to help manage your business on Slotera.

## Viewing Your Staff

Navigate to **Staff** in the sidebar to see all team members associated with your business.

Each staff member's card shows:
- Name and contact information
- Role (Owner, Manager, or Staff)
- When they were added

## Staff Roles

Slotera supports the following roles:

| Role | Permissions |
|------|-------------|
| **Owner** | Full access to all business features, including billing and account deletion. Only one owner per business. |
| **Manager** | Can manage reservations, customers, staff, and business settings. Cannot delete the business or change the owner. |
| **Staff** | Can view and manage reservations and customers. Limited access to business settings. |

## Adding Staff Members

Staff members are added when they register with your business. As the business owner or manager, you can:

1. Go to **Staff** in the sidebar.
2. View all current team members.
3. Update roles as needed.

## Changing a Staff Member's Role

1. Go to **Staff** in the sidebar.
2. Find the staff member you want to update.
3. Click the **Edit** button or role dropdown.
4. Select the new role.
5. Confirm the change.

## Removing a Staff Member

1. Go to **Staff** in the sidebar.
2. Find the staff member you want to remove.
3. Click the **Remove** button.
4. Confirm the removal.

<Callout type="warn">
Removing a staff member revokes their access to your business dashboard. This action cannot be undone—you would need to re-add them if you change your mind.
</Callout>
