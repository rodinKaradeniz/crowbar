# Walk-in Queue

The **Queue** module lets you manage walk-in customers without a reservation.
Each service day must be opened explicitly with a waiting-cover limit before
guests or staff can add parties. Calling a party is separate from whether an
SMS was delivered.

## Enabling the Queue Module

Go to **Settings → Modules** and enable **Queue**. The Queue link will appear in the **Operations** section of the sidebar immediately after saving.

## The Live Queue Board

Navigate to **Queue** in the sidebar. The board shows all active queue entries
in real time. Use **Floor** for the live table and arrival view.

Each entry shows:
- **Name** and **party size**
- **Wait time** since joining
- **Status**: Waiting → Called → Seated (or Removed)
- **Delivery state**: delivered, failed and retryable, or no configured channel

The public queue shows a measured wait estimate only after at least five recent
completed seatings. If there is not enough evidence, Crowbar says so instead of
inventing an estimate.

The sidebar badge next to Queue reflects the current number of waiting parties, refreshed every 30 seconds.

## Queue Actions

| Action | What it does |
|--------|-------------|
| **Call** | Marks the party as called, then attempts an SMS when a channel and phone number are available |
| **Seat at table** | Opens the table selector and creates the actual seating; a party is not seated until registered tables are chosen |
| **Remove** | Requires an operational reason: guest left, no-show, or staff removal |

> **Staff tip:** A called state means staff made the operational call; the
> delivery badge is the evidence for whether a message arrived. Seating changes
> real table occupancy; closing that seating later marks its tables as cleaning
> until staff return them to ready.

## Customer Flow

Customers join the queue by scanning a QR code or visiting your queue URL directly. They enter their name, party size, and phone number — no account required.

Once called, the public status page shows that the table is ready even if an
optional SMS fails. Joining retries safely, and the same active phone number
cannot create duplicate queue parties.
