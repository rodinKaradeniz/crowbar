# Walk-in Queue

The **Queue** module lets you manage walk-in customers without a reservation. Customers join a digital queue and receive an SMS when their table is ready.

## Enabling the Queue Module

Go to **Settings → Modules** and enable **Queue**. The Queue link will appear in the **Operations** section of the sidebar immediately after saving.

## The Live Queue Board

Navigate to **Queue** in the sidebar. The board shows all active queue entries in real time, updated via WebSocket — no page refresh needed.

Each entry shows:
- **Name** and **party size**
- **Wait time** since joining
- **Status**: Waiting → Called → Seated (or Removed)

The sidebar badge next to Queue reflects the current number of waiting parties, refreshed every 30 seconds.

## Queue Actions

| Action | What it does |
|--------|-------------|
| **Call** | Marks the party as called and sends them an SMS: "Your table is ready!" |
| **Seat** | Moves the party to Seated and removes them from the active list |
| **Remove** | Removes the party from the queue (e.g. they left without being seated) |

> **Staff tip:** Only Call and Seat trigger SMS notifications. The acting staff member does not receive their own notification.

## Customer Flow

Customers join the queue by scanning a QR code or visiting your queue URL directly. They enter their name, party size, and phone number — no account required.

Once called, they receive an SMS with your business name. They confirm by tapping "On my way" or simply walk in.
