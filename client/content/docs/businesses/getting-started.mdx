# Getting Started as a Business

Welcome to Crowbar! This guide will help you set up your business and start accepting reservations.

## Creating Your Business Account

1. Visit the [registration page](/auth/register) and select **Business** as your account type.
2. Fill in your business details:
   - **Business Name** — The name customers will see
   - **Your Name** — Your name as the business owner/manager
   - **Email Address** — For login and business communications
   - **Phone Number** — For customer contact
   - **Password** — Secure your account with a strong password
3. Agree to the terms and conditions, then click **Register**.
4. You'll be redirected to your business dashboard.

## Setting Up Your Profile

After registration, complete your business profile to make your listing attractive to customers:

### Business Information

Navigate to **Profile** → **Info** to set up:

- **Business Description** — Tell customers what you offer
- **Address** — Your physical location
- **Contact Information** — Phone, email, and website
- **Business Logo** — Upload your logo for brand recognition
- **Cover Image** — A banner image for your reservation page

### Business Hours

Navigate to **Profile** → **Hours** to configure:

- **Operating Hours** — Set your open and close times for each day of the week
- **Days Off** — Mark days when you're closed
- These are the public venue hours. Reservation availability is managed
  separately under **Profile** → **Booking**.

### Booking Settings

Navigate to **Profile** → **Booking** to configure:

- **Public online bookings** — Choose whether guests can book from your public
  reservation page or whether reservations are staff-only; staff can always
  create and manage reservations.
- **Policy** — Minimum notice, how far ahead customers can book, slot spacing,
  default duration, and the business-wide maximum party size
- **Weekly Hours** — One or more bookable windows per day, including split and
  overnight service
- **Date Exceptions** — Close one date or replace its normal hours
- **Booking Type Overrides** — Let a type inherit the business default or give
  it a complete custom schedule

Owners and managers can edit these settings. Staff members can view them. You
can preview and copy public operating hours into the default weekly booking
hours, but this is a one-time replacement rather than an ongoing sync.

## Your Business Dashboard

The **Overview** page gives you a snapshot of your business:

- **Today's Reservations** — What's coming up today
- **Pending Requests** — Reservation requests awaiting your response
- **Key Stats** — Total reservations, customers, and revenue trends
- **Charts & Trends** — Visual insights into your booking patterns

## Navigating the Platform

Use the sidebar to access the main sections:

- **Overview** — Dashboard with stats and today's schedule
- **Queue** — Walk-in queue management: add parties, call them when ready, track wait times *(visible when the Queue module is enabled)*
- **Orders** — Live kitchen/bar ticket board for real-time order management *(visible when the Ordering module is enabled)*
- **Menu** — Create and manage menus, categories, items, and the item library *(visible when the Ordering module is enabled)*
- **Insights** — AI-powered demand forecasts, customer segments, and cancellation risk (see [Insights](./insights))
- **Docs** — Product guides inside the dashboard (same content as the help assistant)
- **Reservations** — Full list of all bookings
- **Requests** — Pending reservation requests to review
- **Schedule** — Visual timeline of all reservations
- **Customers** — People who have booked with you
- **Staff** — Manage your team members
- **Profile** — Business info, hours, service types, and booking settings
- **Settings** — Account settings, notification channels, module configuration, and embeddable widget

<Callout type="info">
Queue, Ordering, Inventory, and Insights sidebar items only appear when those modules are enabled. Go to **Settings → Modules** to enable or disable any module — changes take effect immediately without reloading the page.
</Callout>

## Your Public Reservation Page

Once your profile is set up, customers can book with you at:

```
/reserve/your-business-slug
```

Share this link on your website, social media, or with customers directly.
