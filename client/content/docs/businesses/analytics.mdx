# Analytics & Insights

The analytics dashboard helps you understand your business performance and make data-driven decisions.

## Accessing Analytics

Your **Overview** page provides key metrics and charts. Here's what you'll find:

## Key Metrics

At a glance, your dashboard shows:

- **Total Reservations** — The total number of bookings your business has received
- **Confirmed Reservations** — How many bookings have been confirmed
- **Total Customers** — The number of unique customers who have booked with you
- **Revenue** — Total revenue from paid reservations (if applicable)

## Charts & Trends

Visual insights help you spot patterns:

### Reservation Trends
A chart showing your reservation volume over time. Use this to:
- Identify your busiest periods
- Spot seasonal trends
- Plan staffing and resources accordingly

### Popular Service Types
See which services are most in demand. This helps you:
- Focus marketing on popular services
- Consider expanding high-demand offerings
- Adjust pricing based on demand

### Customer Activity
Track customer engagement:
- New vs. returning customers
- Booking frequency
- Most active customers

## Using Insights to Grow

Here are some ways to use your analytics:

1. **Optimize your schedule** — If certain time slots are consistently popular, consider extending your hours or adding staff during peak times.
2. **Adjust pricing** — High-demand services might warrant premium pricing, while less popular services could benefit from promotions.
3. **Improve service offerings** — If a service type is rarely booked, consider updating its description, adjusting the price, or replacing it with something more appealing.
4. **Retain customers** — Track returning customers and consider loyalty programs or special offers to keep them coming back.

## Customer Management

Navigate to **Customers** in the sidebar to see everyone who has booked with your business:

- View customer contact information
- See their reservation history
- Track how often they visit
- Identify your most loyal customers

## ML-Powered Insights

For deeper, machine-learning-powered analytics, visit the **Insights** page in the sidebar. The ML pipeline analyzes your historical data to provide:

- **Customer Segmentation** — Automatically groups your customers into segments (Champions, Loyal, At Risk, Lost) based on how recently they booked, how often, and how much they spend.
- **Cancellation Prediction** — Predicts which upcoming reservations are most likely to be cancelled, so you can take proactive action (e.g. send reminders, require deposits).
- **Demand Forecasting** — Forecasts your reservation volume for the next 7 days, helping you plan staffing and resources.

Learn more in the [ML Insights guide](/docs/businesses/insights).
