# Managing Service Types

Service types define what customers can book with your business. For example, a hair salon might have "Haircut", "Coloring", and "Styling" as service types, while a restaurant might have "Table for 2", "Table for 4", and "Private Dining".

## Viewing Your Service Types

Navigate to **Profile** → **Types** to see all your current service types.

## Creating a Service Type

1. Go to **Profile** → **Types**.
2. Click **Add Service Type**.
3. Fill in the **Details** tab:
   - **Name** — The name of the service (e.g., "Haircut", "Consultation")
   - **Description** — A brief description of what the service includes
   - **Duration** — How long the service typically takes (used for scheduling and, for online services, the calendar event length)
   - **Price** — The cost of the service (if applicable)
   - **Capacity** — How many customers can book this service at the same time
   - **Color** — Display color for calendars and lists
4. Optional **Booking behavior**:
   - **Require payment** — When enabled, customers may need to pay a deposit or full amount at booking (Stripe), depending on your configuration.
   - **Online (video) service** — Mark the service as held online. When Google Calendar is connected for your business, Slotera can create a **Google Meet** link and attach it to the reservation and confirmation email. Connect Google from **Profile** → **Types** (connection banner) if you use online services.
   - **Pending requests** — When enabled, new bookings for this service start as **pending** until you accept them in **Requests**. When disabled, eligible bookings are **confirmed** immediately (still subject to your global booking rules).
5. Optional **Custom form** tab — Add extra fields (text, number, select, etc.) that customers must fill when booking this service. Responses are stored on the reservation.
6. Click **Save** to create the service type.

## Google Calendar and Meet

For **online** service types, Meet links are created only if your business has connected **Google Calendar** (OAuth). Use the connect action on **Profile** → **Types** to authorize Slotera. Without a connection, online services still work, but no Meet link is generated automatically.

## Editing a Service Type

1. Go to **Profile** → **Types**.
2. Find the service type you want to edit.
3. Click the **Edit** button.
4. Make your changes and click **Save**.

## Deleting a Service Type

1. Go to **Profile** → **Types**.
2. Find the service type you want to remove.
3. Click the **Delete** button.
4. Confirm the deletion.

<Callout type="warn">
Deleting a service type will not cancel existing reservations of that type, but customers will no longer be able to book it.
</Callout>

## Guests and public booking

Customers booking from your public page can specify **guest count** where the flow supports it. Capacity on the service type helps control how many spots each slot represents.

## Best Practices

- **Be descriptive** — Clear names and descriptions help customers choose the right service.
- **Set accurate durations** — Prevents scheduling conflicts and sets the right length for online calendar events.
- **Keep prices updated** — Ensure your listed prices match your current rates.
- **Review regularly** — Update your service types as your offerings change.
