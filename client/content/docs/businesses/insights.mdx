# Insights

The **Insights** page uses **artificial intelligence** and **machine learning** to go beyond the charts on your Overview. Models learn from your historical reservation data to surface forecasts, segments, and risk signals you can act on.

## Getting Started

Navigate to **Insights** in the sidebar. If this is your first time, you'll see an empty state — click **Run Pipeline** to generate your first set of insights.

The pipeline takes a few seconds to run and will:
1. Load all your reservation and customer data
2. Engineer features (booking patterns, customer behavior, time-series trends)
3. Train three ML models
4. Display the results on the dashboard

## Customer Segmentation

Your customers are automatically grouped into segments using **RFM analysis** (Recency, Frequency, Monetary value) and K-Means clustering:

| Segment | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| **Champions** | Recent, frequent, high-spending customers — your best clients |
| **Loyal Customers** | Regular visitors with consistent booking patterns |
| **Potential Loyalists** | Moderately recent with decent spending — could become loyal |
| **At Risk** | Previously active customers who haven't booked recently |
| **Lost Customers** | Haven't booked in a long time with low frequency |

### How to use this
- **Champions** — Offer exclusive perks or early access to new services
- **At Risk** — Send re-engagement emails or special offers to win them back
- **Lost Customers** — Consider a win-back campaign with a compelling discount

## Cancellation Prediction

A **gradient-boosting** machine learning model (LightGBM) is trained on your resolved reservations (completed vs. cancelled) to predict which future bookings are most likely to be cancelled.

### Key metrics shown
- **AUC-ROC** — How well the model distinguishes cancellations from completions (closer to 1.0 is better)
- **Precision** — Of the reservations flagged as likely cancellations, how many actually were
- **Recall** — Of all actual cancellations, how many the model caught
- **F1 Score** — The harmonic mean of precision and recall

### Top risk factors
The chart shows which features most influence the model's predictions. Common top factors include:
- **Lead time** — Reservations booked far in advance are more likely to be cancelled
- **Payment status** — Paid reservations are much less likely to be cancelled
- **Customer history** — Customers with past cancellations are more likely to cancel again

### How to use this
- Consider requiring deposits for high-risk bookings
- Send confirmation reminders for reservations booked far in advance
- Flag repeat cancellers for special attention

## Demand Forecasting

The demand forecast predicts your expected reservation volume for the next 7 days using historical patterns, day-of-week effects, and rolling averages.

### What you'll see
- **Bar chart** showing predicted daily reservations (weekdays in blue, weekends in purple)
- **Total expected** reservations for the week
- **Busiest / Quietest day** predictions
- **Weekday vs. weekend** averages

### How to use this
- **Staff scheduling** — Schedule more staff on predicted busy days
- **Inventory planning** — Order supplies based on expected volume
- **Marketing** — Run promotions on predicted quiet days to boost traffic

## Running the Pipeline

Click **Run Pipeline** at the top of the Insights page to refresh all predictions with the latest data. The pipeline typically completes in a few seconds.

Results are cached and will persist until you run the pipeline again.
