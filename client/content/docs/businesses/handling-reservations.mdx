# Handling Reservations

Managing reservations efficiently is key to running a successful business on Crowbar.

## Reservation Requests

When a customer books, the reservation may start as **pending** or **confirmed**:

- **Per service type** — On **Profile** → **Types**, each service can enable **Pending requests**. If enabled, new bookings for that service appear under **Requests** until you accept them.
- **Global booking settings** — **Profile** → **Booking** controls availability,
  minimum notice, how far ahead customers can book, slot spacing, and duration.

### Reviewing Requests

1. Navigate to **Requests** in the sidebar.
2. You'll see all pending reservation requests with:
   - Customer name and contact info
   - Requested date and time
   - Service type
   - Any notes from the customer
   - **Custom form** answers, if you configured extra fields on the service type
3. For each request, you can:
   - **Accept** — Confirm the reservation
   - **Reject** — Decline the reservation

<Callout type="info">
Responding to requests promptly improves the customer experience and helps build trust with your clientele.
</Callout>

## Email confirmations and calendar invites

When your environment is configured for outbound email, customers receive a **confirmation email** after a relevant booking is created or updated. That email can include:

- Reservation summary (business, service, time, guests)
- **ICS calendar attachment** — Add the booking to their calendar

## Managing All Reservations

Navigate to **Reservations** in the sidebar to see a complete list of all bookings.

### Filtering and Searching

- **Filter by status** — View only confirmed, pending, completed, or cancelled reservations
- **Search** — Find reservations by customer name, date, or service type
- **Sort** — Order by date, status, or customer name

### Viewing Reservation Details

Click on any reservation to see:
- Full customer information
- Booking details (date, time, service type)
- Notes and special requests
- Reservation history

### Updating a Reservation

Use **Edit** for contact information, notes, or status:

1. Click on the reservation to open its details.
2. Click **Edit**.
3. Update the relevant non-scheduling details.
4. Save your changes.

### Rescheduling a Reservation

Future pending and confirmed reservations have a separate **Reschedule** action
on **Reservations**, **Requests**, and **Schedule**:

1. Choose the booking type and party size.
2. Select a date and one of the available venue-local times Crowbar returns.
3. Review the new booking and confirm the move.

Crowbar keeps the original booking if another user claims the selected slot
first and offers nearby alternatives. Cancelled, completed, and past
reservations cannot be rescheduled. Successful moves send the customer updated
email/calendar details and SMS when those channels are configured.

### Cancelling a Reservation

If you need to cancel a booking:
1. Open the reservation details.
2. Click **Cancel**.
3. Confirm the cancellation.

## Schedule View

The **Schedule** page provides a visual timeline of your reservations:

- See all bookings laid out on a timeline
- Quickly identify busy and available time slots
- Navigate between days to plan ahead
- Click on a reservation in the timeline for quick access to its details

## Auto-Accept vs. Manual Review

You can combine **Profile** → **Booking** defaults with **per–service-type** pending settings:

| Approach | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| **Immediate confirmation** | Disable **Pending requests** on the service type so eligible bookings are confirmed when submitted (subject to availability). |
| **Manual review** | Enable **Pending requests** on services that need approval; use **Requests** to accept or reject. |
