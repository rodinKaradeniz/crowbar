# Account Settings

Keep your account information up to date to ensure a smooth reservation experience.

## Accessing Settings

Click on **Settings** in the sidebar to access your account settings. You'll find two main sections:

### Profile Settings

Update your personal information:

- **Full Name** — How businesses will see your name on reservations
- **Email Address** — Used for login and receiving reservation updates
- **Phone Number** — An alternative way for businesses to contact you

To update your profile:
1. Navigate to **Settings** → **Profile**.
2. Edit the fields you want to change.
3. Click **Save Changes**.

### Account Settings

Manage your account security:

- **Change Password** — Update your password regularly for security
- **Delete Account** — Permanently remove your account and all associated data

## Changing Your Password

1. Go to **Settings** → **Account**.
2. Enter your current password.
3. Enter and confirm your new password.
4. Click **Update Password**.

<Callout type="info">
Your password must be at least 8 characters long. We recommend using a mix of letters, numbers, and special characters.
</Callout>

## Forgot Your Password?

If you've forgotten your password:

1. Go to the [login page](/auth/login).
2. Click **Forgot Password**.
3. Enter your email address.
4. Check your email for a password reset link.
5. Follow the link to set a new password.
