# Getting Started as a Customer

Welcome to Slotera! This guide will walk you through creating your account and making your first reservation.

## Creating Your Account

1. Visit the [registration page](/auth/register) and select **Customer** as your account type.
2. Fill in your details:
   - **Full Name** — Your first and last name
   - **Email Address** — Used for login and reservation confirmations
   - **Phone Number** — So businesses can reach you if needed
   - **Password** — Choose a strong password to secure your account
3. Agree to the terms and conditions, then click **Register**.
4. You'll be redirected to your customer dashboard.

## Your Customer Dashboard

Once logged in, your dashboard gives you a quick overview of:

- **Upcoming Reservations** — Your next scheduled bookings at a glance
- **Recent Activity** — A summary of your latest reservation actions
- **Quick Stats** — Total reservations, upcoming bookings, and more

## Navigating the Platform

Use the sidebar to access the main sections:

- **Overview** — Your dashboard with stats and upcoming reservations
- **Reservations** — View and manage all your bookings
- **Requests** — Track the status of pending reservation requests
- **Settings** — Update your profile and account information
