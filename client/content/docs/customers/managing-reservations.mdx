# Managing Your Reservations

Keep track of all your bookings from your customer dashboard.

## Viewing Your Reservations

Navigate to **Reservations** in the sidebar to see all your bookings. You can:

- **Filter by status** — Show only pending, confirmed, or completed reservations.
- **Search** — Find a specific reservation by business name, date, or service type.
- **Sort** — Order reservations by date, status, or business name.

Each reservation card shows:
- Business name and service type
- Date and time
- Current status
- Any notes you've added

## Viewing Reservation Details

Click on any reservation to see its full details, including:
- Complete booking information
- Business contact details
- Payment status (if applicable)
- Any messages or notes

## Cancelling a Reservation

If you need to cancel a booking:

1. Go to **Reservations** in the sidebar.
2. Find the reservation you want to cancel.
3. Click on the reservation to open its details.
4. Click the **Cancel** button.
5. Confirm the cancellation.

<Callout type="warn">
Cancellation policies vary by business. Some businesses may have specific cancellation windows or fees. Check the business's policy before cancelling.
</Callout>

## Tracking Pending Requests

The **Requests** section shows reservations that are still awaiting a response from the business. Here you can:

- See which requests are still pending
- Check how long ago you submitted a request
- Cancel a pending request if your plans have changed
