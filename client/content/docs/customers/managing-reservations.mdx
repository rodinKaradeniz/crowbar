# Managing Your Reservations

Guests do not create a Crowbar account. Use the private management link sent
for a reservation to review that booking, reconfirm it, move it to another
available time, or cancel it.

## Viewing Your Reservations

Open the reservation-management link from the venue's email or message. The
link is a private bearer credential for that reservation, so do not forward it.

## Viewing Reservation Details

The page shows:
- Complete booking information
- Business contact details
- Any messages or notes

## Cancelling a Reservation

If you need to cancel a booking:

1. Open the reservation-management link.
2. Choose **Cancel reservation**.
3. Confirm the cancellation.

<Callout type="warn">
Late-change rules vary by venue. Crowbar shows when the configured late-change
window applies; contact the venue if service has already started.
</Callout>

If the reservation is pending, the same link continues to show its current
status. A changed or revoked link is intentionally rejected; ask the venue for
help if that happens.
