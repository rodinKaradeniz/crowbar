# Making a Reservation

Booking a reservation on Crowbar is simple and takes just a few minutes.

## Finding a Business

There are several ways to find a business to book with:

1. **Browse the Homepage** — Featured businesses are displayed on the main page in a carousel.
2. **Direct Link** — If a business has shared their reservation page link with you, visit it directly at `/reserve/[business-slug]`.

## Booking Process

Once you've found a business, follow these steps:

### Step 1: Select a Service Type

Choose the type of service you'd like to book. Each business defines their own service types (e.g., "Haircut", "Consultation", "Table for 2").

### Step 2: Choose Date and Time

- Pick your preferred **date** from the calendar.
- Select an available **time slot** based on the business's operating hours.
- The system will only show you times when the business is open and available.

### Step 3: Provide Your Details

Fill in any additional information the business requires, such as:
- Number of **guests**
- Special requests or notes
- Contact preferences

### Step 4: Confirm Your Reservation

Review your booking details and click **Confirm**. You'll see a confirmation screen with your reservation details. You may also receive an **email** with details and a calendar invite.

## Reservation Status

After submitting your reservation, it may have one of these statuses:

| Status | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| **Pending** | Your reservation request has been submitted and is awaiting approval from the business. |
| **Confirmed** | The business has approved your reservation (or the service was set to confirm automatically). |
| **Rejected** | The business was unable to accommodate your request. |
| **Cancelled** | The reservation has been cancelled (by you or the business). |
| **Completed** | The reservation date has passed and the service was completed. |

## Tips for a Smooth Booking

- **Book in advance** — Popular times fill up quickly, so book ahead when possible.
- **Check business hours** — Make sure the business is open on your preferred date.
- **Add notes** — If you have special requirements, include them in the notes field.
- **Keep your contact info updated** — Businesses may need to reach you about your reservation.
